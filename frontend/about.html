About C2T MOBILES

Making second-hand smartphone buying simple, transparent, and trustworthy.

At C2T MOBILES, we believe buying a pre-owned smartphone shouldn't feel like a gamble. Millions of smartphone buyers in India seek high-performing flagship devices like iPhones, Samsung Galaxy, and OnePlus at fair prices, but struggle with hidden defects or automated e-commerce platforms that don't allow personal inspection.

Our Core Principles

  • 30-Point Quality Inspection: Every phone undergoes complete testing covering battery health, cameras, display quality, 5G signals, speakers, and physical frames.
  • Zero Hidden Surprises: We clearly document physical micro-scratches, repairs (if any), and exact battery health percentage upfront.
  • Direct WhatsApp Communication: Talk directly with our owner to request live video inspection, ask questions, and arrange delivery personally before paying a single rupee.
