💡 Things to check before buying a used phone

  • Display & Touch: Verify there are no dead pixels, screen burn-in, or unresponsive touch areas.
  • Battery Health: On iPhones, verify Battery Health percentage under Settings > Battery. On Android, check battery cycle health.
  • Camera Lenses: Test focus, zoom, front camera, portrait mode, and video recording for glass dust or blur.
  • Sensors & Biometrics: Test Face ID, Touch ID / in-display fingerprint sensor responsiveness.
  • Network & SIM: Test physical SIM signal, 5G connectivity, and e-SIM functionality.
