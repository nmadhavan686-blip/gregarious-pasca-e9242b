Last updated: January 2026

1. Business Model

C2T MOBILES sells pre-owned, refurbished, and second-hand smartphones. This website serves as a catalog and enquiry showcase. No direct payment transactions or online checkout processes take place inside this website.

2. WhatsApp Negotiations & Deals

Clicking "Buy on WhatsApp" connects you directly with the store owner. Final sale terms, price, payment mode, delivery method, and warranty duration are agreed upon personally during WhatsApp communication.

3. Device Availability

Each pre-owned device is a unique stock item. Stock status (AVAILABLE / SOLD) is updated by the store administrator. In case a mobile is sold before your enquiry, our team will suggest similar available models.
